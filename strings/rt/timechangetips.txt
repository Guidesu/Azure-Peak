Jackberries are a refreshingly light treat for those that find themselves peckish, while roaming through the bush-laden forests of Vaeltis. A wise traveler knows to always take a testing bite before gorging on the vine's fruits, lest they die with a bitter taste on their tongue.
Pepperberries are the closest relative to the Jackberry fruit, and - when pitted against an untrained eye - virtually indistinguishable from one-another. While a Pepperberry is incredibly toxic when eaten straight off the vine, the spice born from milling its roasted seeds can make for an excellent seasoning.
Allspice is a specially-blended mixture of salt, pepper, sugar, pumpkin spice, and rocknut powder. When properly dried by a well-seasoned culinarian, it can turn even the dreariest broths into a royally delicious stew.
Candies, better known as 'sweetglass', are a popular treat amongst Vaeltis's youth and elders. The most popular recipe, as dictated in the Keep's old cookbooks, calls for a handful of raisins to be smeared in honey and doused in a pot of boiling fat-or-tallow.
Boiling water does not discriminate, when it comes to making stew. Even the most inedible parts of a butchered carcass, like bones and viscera and sinew, can be distilled into broth with enough time.
A stew is only as good as its ingredients - the better they are, the more filling and rich the stew will be. A raw chicken carcass and some whole potatoes might make for a decent broth, but a lavishly-peppered frybird and some roasted potatoes will make for a positively divine stew.
Most food eventually spoils if kept outside of a chest for long enough, unless packed in ration wrapping paper. Just one piece from the Inn is enough to keep even the most extravagent cakes and finemeals fresh until the week's end; a blessing for those who want to subsist off more than hardtack and coppiette, while adventuring abroad.
Milušmas is the most cherished holidae amongst the elders and youth of Vaeltis, where the harshest nites of the winter solstice are countered with freshly-baked pastries and freely-exchanged books. Naturally, Vergenmark's harsher fringe has barred it from being practiced within their valleys, on the grounds of it 'corrupting the youth with unholy thoughts of progress.'
Amongst many pre-Schism denominations of the Tribunal, it is traditional to not invoke the Word's name; for to do so outside of the gravest circumstances would be to dishonor His sacrifice. Instead, they honor Him as 'the Silent Father' - most adrently, through grand and elaborate rituals performed on the winter solstice.
Worship of the Unmaker is rumored, but scarcely proven. Ushering in the complete annihilation of reality, as it turns out, is the only line that not even the most ardent Unveiled or Tribunal-faithful will dare to cross.
The far north's landscape has been irreversably altered by the calamities that had been hosted upon its soil, in the war fought before the Compact.
Better known as the deep frost, the far north is rumored to hide Vaeltis's most well-sought truths beneath its permafrost. The few natives who've embarked southward rarely entertain such rumors, however.
In ancient tymes, worshippers of the bound thing symbolized their faith with a blazing vortex. Purportances of it instead representing the Sun, preceding Auxentius's reign, are harshly punished by the Concordat's scholars.
Whirlwinds, though rare, are unmatched in terms of sheer devastation. Worshippers of the Concordat oft-attribute them as conduits of divine retribution, while Tribunal-faithful fear them as spasms of the Unmaker's leaking magicka.
Corrupted silver burns the unanointed palm, and sunders the lyving without mercy. Such is born from dark magycks, known best by the lyches and necromancers of Aurelian.
Aurelian's Stripping sect has torn down more shrines than anyone can count, and something always seems to go wrong shortly after. In the darkest nites, some say you can see a crimson vortex screaming through the dimmest stars where the latest one fell.
Aurelian sought, in the oldest telling, to save something precious to her from an ailment that would soon doom it to extinction. All she needed was a sliver of the Unmaker's power; yet even she could've never accounted for how weak the boundary was.

Mount Golgatha once hosted the final battle between the mortal vessels of Custodius and Volkovoi. When His blow cleaved Volkovoi's neck asunder, Custodius sought to bury the decapitated remains within its magmatic depths.
Mount Decapitation is always blanketed in a thick layer of snow, owing to its elevation. It cradles many secrets and riches beneath the snow, yet plenty of perils as well.
The Tomb of Alotheos is a massive dungeon, found in the thickets of Mount Decaptation's northern treelines. It is the cause for the Outpost's unusually high amount of visiting Adventurers; after all, a seemingly untouched catacomb with hundreds of treasure-stuffed rooms almost sounds too good to be true.
No one is actually quite sure as to who Alotheos was, though there are plenty of rumors to pick from. RESUSCITATING can be done by targeting someone's mouth while the 'WEAK' combat intent is selected. Breathing air into another can prolong their suffering on this world, and - with the right medicine - bring them back.
SHIFT-CLICKING with the RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON allows you to look farther ahead in a chosen direction. The higher your PERCEPTION is, the further that you can peer into the distance.
CLICKING with the MIDDLE MOUSE BUTTON allows you to perform a SUBINTENT action, highlighted to the LEFT of your HANDS. Make sure to leave them unselected, if you happen to be in the business of butchering carcasses.
JUMPING is performed by MIDDLE-CLICKING a chosen tile while the 'JUMP' subintent is selected. With it, you can leap over tables and smaller gaps in the terrain.
LEAPING is performed by MIDDLE-CLICKING a chosen tile while both the 'JUMP' subintent and 'RUN' button are enabled. This covers far more ground than a traditional hop, but comes at the cost of a harsher landing.
KICKING is performed by MIDDLE-CLICKING someone while the 'KICK' subintent is selected. Besides knocking over washbins and busting doors down, successfully kicking a foe into a structure will always topple them over.
BITING is performed by MIDDLE-CLICKING someone while the 'BITE' subintent is selected. Flowing streams can be sipped from, while those with a more humanitarian diet can use this to indulge in veiny treats.
SPECIALS are performed by MIDDLE-CLICKING a chosen tile while the 'SPECIAL' subintent is selected. If you're skilled enough with a weapon, you can use this to unleash an especially powerful attack - mind the cooldown.
TRIUMPHS can be earned through either surviving a week's trials, or by completing whatever tribulations sprout up along the way. These can be exchanged in the LOADOUT for a wide variety of cosmetic decorations.
VICES are an unavoidable part of lyfe, and - if left unfulfilled - can utterly ruin your mood. Those riding off the high of a recent TRIUMPH can temporarily smother their VICE for a week's tyme.. at a cost.
VIRTUES embody a special quirk that differentiates your actor from the rest of the rabble. Those who've completely surrendered themselvse to the whims of fate and causality can embody TWO VIRTUES, instead of just ONE.
STATPACKS bolster the statistical power of your actor. While this can allow for some truly legendary feats, one must always remember that great power always come at a greater cost.
MOODLETS sway the outcome of any given week. Treating yourself right and routinely indulging in your VICES will leave you unfettered by even the most discouraging sights. Neglect yourself for too long, however, and..
STRENGTH represents your actor's raw might. This affects the lethality of their strikes with fists and blades, how quickly they can struggle out of a tight spot, and how effectively they can overpower others.
WILLPOWER represents your actor's mental fortitude. This affects the threshold of how much pain they can undertake before collapsing, and how severely their mood is swayed in the face of trouble-and-trauma.
CONSTITUTION represents your actor's physical resilience. This affects the health of each individual body part, how much blood can be spilled before succumbing, and their ability to resist debilitating strikes.
PERCEPTION represents your actor's keen-eyedness. This affects the accuracy of their strikes, the lethality of their shots with bows and slings, and their chances of successfully discovering hidden surprises.
INTELLIGENCE represents your actor's wisdom. This affects the potency of their magicka, the preciseness of assessing another's skills-and-weaknesses, and their chances of successfully performing unfamiliar tasks.
SPEED represents your actor's fleet-footedness. This affect the haste of their movements, their proficiency with many underhanded tactics, and their chances of successfully dodging oncoming attacks.
FORTUNE represents your actor's luck. This affects their chance to sway seemingly random events in their favor, or - in the inverse - against their favor.
